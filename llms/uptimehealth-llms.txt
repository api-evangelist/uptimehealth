# UptimeHealth

> UptimeHealth is a healthcare operations platform for managing medical equipment service, compliance documentation, and facility maintenance across dental, medical, and veterinary practices from a single dashboard. It tracks devices, automates compliance tasks and logs, and coordinates repair service requests; the company reports 73,000+ devices protected across 6,000+ facilities. UptimeHealth publishes no public developer API, SDKs, or API documentation — its platform (uptimecontroller.com) is a closed SaaS application.
